# Provider Policies

> Rules for including certifications from each provider in the EBS AI Passport.

## Provider-by-Provider Policies

### Forage (59 certifications)

- **Access model**: University partnership or individual access. Forage works with universities to provide virtual internship simulations.
- **Cost to students**: Free (via EBS partnership or direct).
- **Badge type**: Certificate of completion with company name.
- **Display rules**: Company name prominent, Forage secondary. Example: "Deloitte Data Analytics Simulation (Forage)."
- **Academic integration**: High — simulations are practical, hands-on, and assessable.
- **EBS policy**: Forage simulations count as applied assessments. Not a replacement for certification exams.

### IBM SkillsBuild (44 certifications)

- **Access model**: Free via IBM SkillsBuild platform. No credit card required.
- **Cost to students**: Free.
- **Badge type**: Digital badges via Credly.
- **Display rules**: Student-earned Credly badges can be displayed. IBM logo cannot be used in EBS marketing without partnership.
- **Academic integration**: Good — structured learning paths, many AI-related.
- **EBS policy**: Core provider for AI literacy and AI engineering certs. Apply for institutional access.

### HubSpot Academy (31 certifications)

- **Access model**: Free via HubSpot Academy. No restrictions.
- **Cost to students**: Free.
- **Badge type**: HubSpot Academy certifications.
- **Display rules**: Widely displayed by learners. Permissive for badge display.
- **Academic integration**: Good for marketing/CRM tracks.
- **EBS policy**: Primary provider for marketing and CRM certifications. 21 of 31 links are generic and need fixing before student-space launch.
- **Issue**: 21 links point to `academy.hubspot.com` (generic) instead of specific course pages.

### Google (25 certifications)

- **Access model**: Free via Google Digital Garage / Google Skillshop / Coursera.
- **Cost to students**: Free (Google Digital Garage) or via Coursera subscription.
- **Badge type**: Google Career Certificates (Coursera), Google Digital Garage certificates, Google Skillshop badges.
- **Display rules**: Google logo use restricted to authorized partners. Display student-earned badges only.
- **Academic integration**: High — FIBAA ECTS credit recommendations available since Jan 2024.
- **EBS policy**: Apply for Google for Education partnership. Use FIBAA framework to credit Google Career Certificates as for-credit modules.

### Semrush (25 certifications)

- **Access model**: Free via Semrush Academy.
- **Cost to students**: Free.
- **Badge type**: Semrush certifications.
- **Display rules**: Permissive — widely displayed by learners.
- **Academic integration**: Good for marketing/SEO tracks.
- **EBS policy**: Primary provider for SEO and digital marketing analytics certifications.

### Cisco Networking Academy (23 certifications)

- **Access model**: Institutional — EBS would need to become a Cisco Networking Academy.
- **Cost to students**: Free via institutional membership.
- **Badge type**: Cisco Networking Academy certificates.
- **Display rules**: Official academy partnership required for logo use. Student certificates can be displayed.
- **Academic integration**: Excellent for cyber and technical tracks.
- **EBS policy**: Apply for Cisco Networking Academy membership. Required for cyber programme integration.

### Harvard via edX (13 certifications)

- **Access model**: Paid (edX verified certificate) or free (audit). Some scholarships available.
- **Cost to students**: $50-300 per certificate (unless EBS secures institutional discount).
- **Academic integration**: High quality but expensive. Use selectively for master-level deep dives.
- **EBS policy**: Do not imply EBS-Harvard partnership. Display as "Harvard via edX." Consider bulk institutional access for cost reduction.

### Google Career Certificates (9 certifications)

- **Access model**: Paid via Coursera ($49/month subscription). Financial aid available.
- **Cost to students**: $49/month (Coursera subscription) or free via financial aid.
- **Badge type**: Google Career Certificates (Coursera).
- **Display rules**: Same as Google above. FIBAA ECTS credit recommendations available.
- **Academic integration**: Excellent — can be credited as 6-9 ECTS per certificate.
- **EBS policy**: Strong candidate for formal academic credit integration. Use FIBAA framework.

### Microsoft Learn (7 certifications)

- **Access model**: Free training via Microsoft Learn. Paid exams for certification.
- **Cost to students**: Free (training) / $100-165 (exam).
- **Badge type**: Microsoft certifications (via Pearson VUE exams).
- **Display rules**: Student-earned certifications can be displayed. Microsoft logo restricted.
- **Academic integration**: Good — FIBAA ECTS recommendations for 7 certificates.
- **EBS policy**: Use for Azure/AI related certifications. Consider institutional exam vouchers.

### Other Providers (Databricks, Scrum, DeepLearning.AI, Bloomberg, LinkedIn, MSI, Fortinet, PMI, n8n, Ahrefs)

- **General policy**: All free certifications are welcome. Paid certifications need institutional cost justification.
- **Display rules**: Student-earned badges/certificates only. No EBS branding with provider logos.
- **Academic integration**: Varies — Scrum/PMI for project management, Bloomberg for finance, Databricks for data engineering.
