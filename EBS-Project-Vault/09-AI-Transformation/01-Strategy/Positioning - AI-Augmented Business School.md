# Positioning - AI-Augmented Business School

## Positioning Concept

EBS can position itself as a business school where AI is integrated into every academic and professional pathway.

## Core Message

> L'IA n'est pas une option chez EBS. C'est une compétence transversale intégrée à chaque parcours.

## Proof Points

- AI Passport for all students.
- Curated international certification pathways.
- Domain-specific AI workflows.
- Applied projects and portfolio.
- Future Wall of Badges with verified student achievements.

## Audience-Specific Framing

### Students

Graduate with practical AI skills, international badges, and portfolio evidence.

### Parents

EBS prepares students for modern employability, not only academic theory.

### Employers

EBS graduates can use AI tools in real professional workflows.

### EBS Administration

The programme differentiates EBS in a crowded private higher education market.
