# Success Metrics

## Year 1 Targets (Pilot Cohort 2026-2027)

### Academic Metrics

| Metric | Target | Measurement |
|---|---|---|
| Programmes with AI Passport mapping | 9/9 (100%) | All programmes have mandatory/optional classifications assigned. |
| Faculty modules integrating AI workflows | 50% of modules | At least half of modules per programme reference AI tools or assignments. |
| Mandatory AI Literacy completion rate (Licence) | 70%+ | Students completing the active licence AI literacy core. |
| Mandatory AI Literacy completion rate (Master) | 75%+ | Students completing the active master AI literacy core. |
| Portfolio project completion rate | 60%+ | Students producing at least one AI-assisted portfolio artifact. |

### Certification Metrics

| Metric | Target | Measurement |
|---|---|---|
| Mandatory certification completion rate | 70%+ | Across all programmes. |
| Verified badges submitted | 100+ in Year 1 | Badge verification workflow active. |
| Provider link validity rate | 95%+ | Quarterly validation of managed catalogue URLs. |
| Optional certification uptake | 20% of students complete at least 1 optional cert | Engagement signal from ambitious students. |
| HubSpot link fix rate | 100% before student-space launch | All HubSpot records marked with `sourceNote` resolved. |

### Website/Product Metrics

| Metric | Target | Measurement |
|---|---|---|
| Visits to /certifications and /ia-et-certifications | 5,000+ visits/year | GA4 analytics. |
| Preinscription conversions from AI/certification pages | 15% of all conversions | UTM tracking on AI-related CTAs. |
| Engagement with programme certification sections | 30% of programme page visitors scroll to certifications section | Scroll depth tracking. |
| Wall of Badges launch | MVP with 10-20 verified students | End of Year 1. |

### Reputation Metrics

| Metric | Target | Measurement |
