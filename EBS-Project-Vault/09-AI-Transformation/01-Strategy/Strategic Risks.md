# Strategic Risks

## Risk: Certification Overload

The roadmap lists very high totals for some paths. If presented as mandatory, students and faculty may reject the programme.

Mitigation: use the managed catalogue with `mandatory` and `optional` requirements, and keep the raw 267-item extraction as reference only.

## Risk: Certificate-Centered Positioning

If EBS presents only certificate counts, the initiative may feel superficial.

Mitigation: frame as AI Passport + portfolio + badges.

## Risk: Link Rot

External certification URLs may change.

Mitigation: maintain a link validation log and avoid public direct links.

## Risk: Faculty Adoption

Faculty may not feel prepared to integrate AI assignments.

Mitigation: faculty enablement and clear module ownership.

## Risk: Unverified Badge Claims

Students may claim badges without proof.

Mitigation: badge verification workflow and student space upload process.
