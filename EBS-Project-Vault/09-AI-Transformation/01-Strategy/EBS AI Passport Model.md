# EBS AI Passport Model

## Model Overview

The AI Passport has three layers.

## Layer 1: AI Core

Mandatory transversal foundation for all students.

Topics:

- AI literacy
- Prompting
- AI productivity
- Data literacy
- Ethics and responsible AI
- AI limits, hallucinations, and verification

## Layer 2: AI for My Profession

Programme-specific application.

Examples:

- Management: AI for operations, decision support, entrepreneurship.
- Marketing: AI for content, SEO, analytics, campaigns, CRM.
- Finance: AI for Excel, forecasting, risk, data analysis.
- Software/IA: ML, agents, data science, app building.
- Cybersecurity: AI for threat intelligence, SOC workflows, cloud security.

## Layer 3: AI Portfolio

Students produce applied work that proves capability.

Examples:

- Marketing campaign dashboard.
- Financial forecasting model.
- AI-powered business process automation.
- SOC/threat analysis workflow.
- AI agent or application prototype.

## Certification Load Guidance

Current active source of truth uses `mandatory` and `optional`, not the older `required/recommended/optional` split.

| Level | Mandatory | Recommended | Optional |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Licence | 11-14 mandatory across programme | Deferred | Managed optional catalogue |
| Master | 13-14 mandatory across programme | Deferred | Managed optional catalogue |

See [[../02-Certifications/Managed Catalogue Review]] for current counts.

## Public Claim Guidance

Prefer:

- `100+ certification opportunities`
- `120+ managed certification pathways`
- `150+ certification opportunities`
- `AI Passport included in every programme`
- `International badges and applied portfolio`

Avoid:

- `Every student completes 200 certifications`
- `55 mandatory certifications`
