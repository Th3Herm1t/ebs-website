# AI Transformation North Star

## North Star Statement

EBS becomes an AI-augmented business school where every student, regardless of specialization, graduates with AI literacy, domain-specific AI workflows, verified badges, and an applied portfolio.

## What This Is

- A modernization of EBS academic positioning.
- A student employability strategy.
- A website and student-space product strategy.
- A certification-backed proof layer.
- A faculty enablement programme.
- A competitive differentiator among Tunisian business schools.

## What This Is Not

- A dumping ground for hundreds of online certificates.
- A replacement for faculty-led academic modules.
- A mandatory requirement to finish 30-55 certifications per student.
- A public link farm to external provider websites.
- An AI degree — it is an AI Passport layered onto existing degrees.

## Strategic Narrative

EBS integrates AI across management, marketing, finance, software, cybersecurity, CRM, project management, and financial engineering. Students learn how to use AI in their profession, prove it through projects, and validate it through international badges.

## Positioning Line Draft

> EBS forme des profils augmentés par l'IA: diplôme reconnu, compétences métier, badges internationaux et portfolio appliqué.

## Three Pillars

### 1. AI Literacy (Every Student)

- Foundational AI concepts (what is ML, NLP, generative AI).
- Prompt engineering and AI-assisted productivity.
- AI ethics and responsible use.
- **Delivered via**: Mandatory AI Literacy core in the managed catalogue (5 licence AI literacy certs, 4 master AI literacy certs).

### 2. Domain AI Workflows (By Programme)

- Marketing students use AI for SEO, content, ads.
- Finance students use AI for forecasting, risk analysis.
- Cyber students use AI for threat detection, SOC operations.
- **Delivered via**: Programme-specific mandatory + optional certifications.

### 3. Verified Proof (Portfolio + Badges)

- Portfolio artifacts demonstrating real AI application.
- Verified badges from international providers.
- Public Wall of Badges for social proof.
- **Delivered via**: Portfolio model + badge verification workflow.

## Success Criteria

| Criterion | Target | Timeline |
|---|---|---|
| All 9 programmes have AI Passport mapping | 100% | Now ✅ |
| Pilot cohort completes required certs | 70%+ | End of Year 1 |
| Faculty modules integrate AI workflows | 50%+ | End of Year 1 |
| Wall of Badges MVP launched | 10-20 students | End of Year 1 |
| EBS recognised as AI-augmented business school | Market perception | Year 2 |

## Competitive Moat

1. **First-mover**: No Tunisian business school has a branded AI Passport.
2. **Multi-vendor**: 121 managed public-visible certification records, curated from a larger 267-item raw extraction.
3. **Structured**: Classified by AI Literacy, Applied AI, and Non-AI, with mandatory/optional requirements per programme.
4. **Verified**: Badge verification + portfolio proof.
5. **Public**: Wall of Badges creates social proof.
6. **Faculty-integrated**: Not just a MOOC list — pedagogical scaffolding.
