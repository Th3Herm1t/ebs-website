# Benchmark Competitors

> What direct competitor universities in Tunisia and the region are doing with AI integration.

## Tunisian Business School Competitors

### ESPRIT (Private Engineering)

- **Offering**: Diplôme d'Ingénieur with "Ingénieur en Intelligence Artificielle" option (3-year cycle, EURACE accredited).
- **AI integration**: Dedicated AI track within engineering degree.
- **Certifications**: No visible external certification integration (Google/IBM/HubSpot etc.).
- **Gap vs EBS**: No business school track. No certification portfolio. No "passport" concept.

### Université Centrale (Private Multi-Disciplinary)

- **Offering**: Mastère Professionnel "Ingénierie en IA" + Big Data + Cyber. CRCI research centre (Oct 2025).
- **AI integration**: Dedicated mastère + research infrastructure.
- **Certifications**: No visible external certification integration.
- **Gap vs EBS**: Technical focus only. No business/management integration. No student-facing badge system.

### ESGITECH (Private, Le Kram)

- **Offering**: Mastère Professionnel "IA et Data Science" — 7500 TND/year.
- **AI integration**: Single mastère programme.
- **Certifications**: Not visible.
- **Gap vs EBS**: Narrow scope. No cross-disciplinary AI. No certification tiering.

### UIT (Private)

- **Offering**: IGL with "Data Science-IA" and "Ingénierie des Systèmes Intelligents et IoT" options. SAP certifications included.
- **AI integration**: Specialization tracks within engineering.
- **Certifications**: SAP (only vendor cert visible).
- **Gap vs EBS**: No multi-vendor certification portfolio. No passport model.

## African Competitors

### ALX / African Leadership University

- **Offering**: AI Career Essentials (AiCE) — 6 weeks, certificate.
- **AI integration**: Short-form standalone programme.
- **Certifications**: Completion certificate only (no vendor badges).
- **Gap vs EBS**: Short-form only. No degree integration. No portfolio stacking.

### AIMS + Google DeepMind

- **Offering**: Master "AI for Science" + AMMI (fully funded).
- **AI integration**: Research-intensive, selective.
- **Certifications**: Google partnership but research-focused, not certification.
- **Gap vs EBS**: Research-only. Not accessible to business students.

### UTAMU (Uganda)

- **Offering**: Postgraduate Diploma in AI & Data Science (1 year, online).
- **AI integration**: Online postgraduate diploma.
- **Certifications**: Not visible.
- **Gap vs EBS**: Online-only. No in-person component. No multi-vendor certs.
