# Competitive Landscape

> As of July 2026. Sources: university websites, it-news.tn, coursera.org, Coursera/FIBAA reports.

## Key Finding

**No Tunisian university or business school has branded an "AI Passport" model.** This is white space. EBS can claim first-mover advantage among Tunisian business schools with a structured, certification-backed AI credential programme.

| Institution | Type | What They Offer | EBS Differentiation |
|---|---|---|---|
| Université Centrale | Private | Mastère Professionnel "Ingénierie en IA"; Big Data & Data Science mastère | Technical focus — no business/management track |
| ESPRIT | Private | Ingénieur Informatique with IA option | Engineering degree, no business integration |
| ESGITECH | Private | Mastère Professionnel "IA et Data Science" | Narrow AI/DA focus, no cross-disciplinary |
| UIT | Private | IGL with IA specialization | Technical engineering, not business |
| SUP'COM | Public | Cybersecurity mastère, AI in doctoral research | Research/specialist, not business |
| ENSIT | Public | Mastère Recherche "Systèmes Intelligents" | Research M2, no business |
| Université Centrale (CRCI) | Private | Research centre for AI + digital transition (Oct 2025) | Research infrastructure, not student certification |
| **EBS** | **Private** | **Master Digital Marketing & AI (2024)**, **Master CRM**, **AI DAY (May 2026)**, **AI Passport initiative** | **Only Tunisian business school with certification-integrated AI programme** |

**EBS advantage**: Other Tunisian institutions offer AI in technical engineering tracks. EBS is the only business school embedding industry AI certifications (Google, IBM, Cisco, HubSpot, etc.) into management/business/cyber programmes at both licence and master level.

## MOOC-to-University Credit (Critical Enabler)

Since January 2024, the **FIBAA ECTS framework** enables universities in all 49 Bologna Process countries (including Tunisia) to award formal credit for:

- Google Career Certificates (6-9 ECTS)
- IBM Professional Certificates (4-8 ECTS)
- Microsoft Professional Certificates (3-8 ECTS)
- Meta Professional Certificates (ECTS eligible)

**This means EBS can formally credit Google, IBM, and Microsoft certifications as for-credit modules** — a differentiation no other Tunisian private university appears to be leveraging.

### Reference Models

- **University of Europe for Applied Sciences (UE Germany)**: Embeds free HubSpot, IBM, AWS, Facebook, Tableau certifications as acceleration courses in degrees since 2021.
- **WGU + HubSpot**: Marketing AI Micro-Internship Programme with certificate + $500 stipend (Fall 2025).
- **University of Texas System / University of Michigan**: Google Career Certificates embedded as for-credit modules.
