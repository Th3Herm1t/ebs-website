# AI in Higher Education Notes

> Research themes and observations relevant to EBS AI Passport programme design.

## AI Literacy as a Graduate Attribute

- Multiple universities now list "AI literacy" alongside traditional digital literacy.
- The European Commission's DigComp framework is being extended to include AI competencies.
- EBS can position the AI Passport as EBS's answer to "AI literacy" graduate attribute.

## Academic Integrity and AI-Assisted Work

- Assessment design must evolve to be "AI-resilient" — not AI-proof.
- Process documentation + oral defense > take-home exams.
- Portfolio artifacts that require iteration are harder to fake with AI.
- See [[../03-Academic-Design/Assessment Model]].

## Assessment Redesign

- Traditional exams are increasingly unreliable as proof of knowledge.
- Simulation-based assessment (Forage model) is gaining traction.
- Badge-verified competency is a valid complement to grades.
- EBS should combine: certification (external) + applied assignment (internal) + portfolio (integrative) + reflection (metacognitive).

## Portfolio-Based Proof

- Employers increasingly value demonstrable projects over grades.
- The "Wall of Badges" concept creates public accountability and social proof.
- Portfolio artifacts should be assessable, not just decorative.

## Faculty Enablement

- Without faculty buy-in, AI Passport is just a list of online courses.
- Faculty need training on: AI tools, assessment design, academic integrity, portfolio evaluation.
- Monthly peer learning sessions during pilot are essential.
- See [[../03-Academic-Design/Faculty Enablement]].

## Student Employability

- AI skills are now listed in 40%+ of entry-level job postings in marketing, finance, and tech.
- Tunisian employers are beginning to expect AI familiarity.
- The AI Passport gives EBS graduates a concrete, verifiable differentiator.
- The portfolio artifact + badge wall creates a LinkedIn-ready profile.

## Key Trends (2025-2026)

1. **Micro-credentials stacking**: Universities moving toward stackable short-form credentials that count toward degrees.
2. **FIBAA ECTS framework**: Google/IBM/Microsoft certificates now formally credit-eligible in 49 Bologna Process countries.
3. **AI simulation assessments**: Forage, Cloudinary, and others replacing traditional case studies.
4. **Digital badge visibility**: LinkedIn, Credly, and institutional badge walls becoming standard.
5. **Faculty AI adoption lagging**: Most universities have student-facing AI initiatives but limited faculty training.
