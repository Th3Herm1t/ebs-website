## Tunisian Employer Expectations (Emerging)

- AI familiarity is appearing in entry-level job postings for marketing, finance, and tech roles.
- Employers increasingly expect graduates to have demonstrable digital skills, not just theoretical knowledge.
- Portfolio proof (GitHub, LinkedIn badges, live projects) is becoming a differentiator in hiring.
- Tunisian startups and SMEs are adopting AI tools (ChatGPT, Gemini, Copilot) for productivity — graduates who can use these effectively have an advantage.
