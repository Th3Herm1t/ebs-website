# Wall of Badges Spec

## Strategic Purpose

The Wall of Badges creates social proof that EBS students actually complete and share international credentials.

## Public Display

- Student name/photo only with consent.
- Programme and year.
- Verified badges.
- Provider logos.
- Date completed.

## Verification

- Student submits badge/certificate.
- Staff validates.
- Student grants public display consent.
- Badge appears on Wall of Badges.

## Risks

- Privacy.
- Fake badge submissions.
- Stale achievements.

## Minimum Viable Version

Manual curated wall with 10-20 verified students after pilot cohort.
