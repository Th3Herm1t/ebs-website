# Glossary

## AI Passport

The proposed EBS-wide skill framework: a curated set of AI literacy, AI productivity, domain-specific AI workflows, and portfolio evidence completed by students across their academic path.

## Certification Catalogue

The full library of potential certifications and badges. It should not be treated as a mandatory student workload.

## Mandatory Certification

A certification that every student in a target programme/cohort must complete.

## Recommended Certification

Legacy/deferred requirement level. The active managed catalogue currently uses only `mandatory` and `optional`.

## Optional Certification

Catalogue item for ambitious students, clubs, career office challenges, or specialization tracks.

## Forage Simulation

A professional simulation hosted through Forage. Public display should foreground the company/provider, with `*Simulation Forage` as a secondary note.

## Wall of Badges

Future public/social-proof feature showing verified student badges with consent.
