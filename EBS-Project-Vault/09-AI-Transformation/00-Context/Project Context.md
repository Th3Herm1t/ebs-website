# Project Context

## People and Organizations

- **Abdelkarim**: INTP business solutions architect, working at Propulsia.ai, responsible for much of the architecture and implementation work.
- **Ahmed**: long-term EBS project contact and primary communication interface between Propulsia.ai and EBS. He recognized Abdelkarim's polymath potential and helped bring him into long-term work.
- **Raouf**: EBS owner/client context. First major client around which the Propulsia.ai relationship formed.
- **Propulsia.ai**: company/venture created from the longer-term collaboration around EBS work.
- **EBS / Espima Business School**: Tunisian private university and client.

## Origin Story

Abdelkarim initially built a website for a client as a freelance engagement. The client, Raouf, was connected to EBS. Ahmed acted as the project contact between Abdelkarim and Raouf/EBS. Ahmed noticed Abdelkarim's potential and hired him long term. This collaboration evolved into Propulsia.ai, with EBS as the first client.

## Current Initiative

Ahmed and Abdelkarim decided to modernize EBS by incorporating AI as a transversal skill for all students and branches. The initial idea is to incorporate free and currently available certified online resources into EBS programmes.

## Current Artifacts

- A large HTML certification roadmap exists.
- Ahmed provided initial recommendations about public website display, provider logos, Forage treatment, HubSpot links, student space, and Wall of Badges.
- The EBS website has already been modernized significantly in Next.js.

## Strategic Reframe

The raw certification list should become an academic/product system:

- AI Passport
- Certification tiers
- Programme-specific pathways
- Portfolio/capstone layer
- Public website catalogue
- Future student space
- Badge verification workflow
