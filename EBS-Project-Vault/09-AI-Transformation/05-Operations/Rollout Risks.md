# Rollout Risks

## Risk Register

| Risk | Likelihood | Impact | Mitigation |
|---|---|---|---|
| Student overload | High | High | Use managed catalogue mandatory counts as the current ceiling. Cohort/semester enforcement is deferred. |
| Faculty resistance | High | High | Faculty enablement workshops. Monthly check-ins. Start with willing faculty. |
| Broken provider links | High | Medium | Quarterly batch crawl. Fix broken links before student-space launch. |
| Unclear mandatory requirements | Medium | High | Active model uses `mandatory` and `optional`; avoid reintroducing `recommended` until operations require it. |
| Public overpromising | Medium | High | Frame as "AI Passport" not "AI degree." Use "students earn" not "EBS certifies." Validate all claims with Raouf. |
| No verification workflow | Medium | Medium | Badge verification workflow defined. Start with manual Google Form; automate later. |
| Badge wall privacy issues | Medium | Medium | Opt-in only. No student data published without consent. |
| Provider policy changes | Low | Medium | Monitor provider terms quarterly. Have fallback certifications identified. |
| EBS administration resistance | Medium | High | Raouf buy-in is critical. Show employer feedback and student demand data. |
| Accreditation concerns | Low | High | Certifications supplement, not replace, accredited curriculum. Document integration clearly. |
| Partner dependency (Propulsia) | Low | Medium | Ensure knowledge transfer to EBS. Document all processes in this vault. |

## Mitigation Strategy

1. **Start with a pilot**: Launch with 2-3 programmes (e.g., Licence Marketing + Licence Info IA + Master Marketing & IA).
2. **Keep mandatory load bounded**: current managed range is 11-14 mandatory records per programme; semester rollout is deferred.
3. **Validate links quarterly**: Batch-crawl all URLs, fix broken links.
4. **Frame the public story**: "EBS AI Passport" + portfolio, not "267 certifications."
5. **Faculty-first**: Enable faculty before launching to students.
6. **Opt-in badge wall**: No student data published without explicit consent.
