## Goal

Every master student applies AI to advanced professional workflows and produces portfolio evidence.

## Student Outcomes

- Use AI for discipline-specific decision support.
- Understand agents and automation potential.
- Build or specify AI-enabled business workflows.
- Evaluate AI risks, governance, and data implications.
- Produce a capstone or professional-grade portfolio artifact.

## Notes

- M1 focuses on GenAI foundations and agentic AI (4 required).
- M2 focuses on applied build/deploy capstone (1 required).
- The jump from licence to master is significant: from "use AI tools" to "build and deploy AI systems."
- Agentic AI (CERT-024, CERT-028) is the differentiator — most business schools do not teach this yet.
- Forage simulations in optional tier provide employer-brand exposure (Datacom, EAB).
