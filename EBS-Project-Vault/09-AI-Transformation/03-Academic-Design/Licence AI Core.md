> The transversal AI block applied to ALL licence programmes.
> 22 total certs → 7 required, 8 recommended, 7 optional.

## Goal

Every licence student gains practical AI literacy and responsible AI usage skills regardless of specialization.

## Student Outcomes

- Understand what AI can and cannot do.
- Use prompting for study and professional productivity.
- Use AI tools with verification and critical thinking.
- Understand basic data concepts.
- Build one simple AI-assisted workflow.

## Notes

- L1 focuses on AI literacy and prompting (3 required).
- L2 focuses on deeper application and data literacy (3 required).
- L3 focuses on applied project (1 required capstone).
- Recommended certs are for students who want to go deeper but are not mandatory.
- Optional certs are available in the catalogue for ambitious students, clubs, and challenges.
