# Faculty Enablement

## Need

Faculty must understand how to integrate AI without turning modules into random online-course lists. The AI Passport fails if faculty simply say "go do these certificates" without pedagogical scaffolding.

## Enablement Programme

### Workshop 1: AI Passport Overview (2 hours)

- What the AI Passport is and why EBS is doing it.
- Mandatory vs optional requirements and AI Literacy / Applied AI / Non-AI classification.
- How to read the [[../03-Academic-Design/Programme Mapping]].
- Q&A on student workload.

### Workshop 2: AI Tools Hands-On (3 hours)

- Using ChatGPT/Claude for teaching preparation.
- Creating AI-assisted assignments.
- Prompt engineering for educators.
- Demonstrating at least one tool from each provider (Google, IBM, Microsoft, Cisco).

### Workshop 3: Assessment Design (2 hours)

- Designing applied assignments that align with certifications.
- Using the [[Assessment Model]] 4-layer approach.
- Building rubrics from the [[Portfolio and Capstone Model]] template.
- Avoiding "certificate counting" grading.

### Workshop 4: Academic Integrity and AI (1.5 hours)

- Setting expectations for AI use in coursework.
- Detecting AI-generated submissions vs AI-assisted work.
- Designing assessments that are AI-resilient (oral defense, process documentation).
- University policy on AI use.
